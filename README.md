# The Work AI Leaves Behind

If AI resolves half your contacts, can you assume you need half the support team?

I built this calculator to explore what happens to the work left for people: how long it takes, the time spent checking AI, the help AI gives reps, and what happens when contact volume grows.

The article walks through a scenario alongside the tool. Change the assumptions and see what happens to workload and cost.
