Say your team handles 10,000 contacts a week and you route some to AI agents. The quick way to size the team is to take the contacts left over and divide by your average handle time. That number runs low every time. AI agents clear the easy contacts first, so what survives for human agents is the hard, slow work. Push coverage all the way up and you still don’t reach zero. There’s a floor of human agents you always need.
AI moves your headcount two ways at once. Every contact AI resolves still needs a human agent to check it, which pushes the floor up. At the same time, AI assists your team on the contacts they keep, which pulls it back down. Set both levers below and watch where the floor settles once they net out.
For most teams the real win from AI isn’t a smaller team. It’s handling more contacts without hiring at the same pace. Set your expected volume growth, switch to the After-growth view, and compare the human agents you’d need with AI against the number you’d need without it.
