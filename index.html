Say your team handles 10,000 contacts a week and you route some to AI. The obvious way to estimate headcount: take the remaining contacts, divide by average handle time. But that misses something — AI resolves the easy tickets first. What’s left for humans is disproportionately hard, so the real handle time per ticket goes up. You always need more humans than the simple math suggests.
Every ticket AI resolves still creates human work: QA checks, recontact monitoring, handoff audits, knowledge-base fixes. Set the oversight minutes per AI-resolved contact below. Even at 100% AI with perfect resolution, your team isn’t zero — someone is supervising the machine.
Not every AI interaction is pass/fail. AI can assist the human during the interaction — surfacing customer context, suggesting solutions, auto-filling details, summarizing prior interactions — so the human resolves it faster. This works the same across email, chat, and voice. Set how many of your human-handled contacts get AI assistance, and how much faster they go.
The real value of AI in most support orgs isn’t cutting headcount — it’s absorbing volume growth without proportional hiring. Set a growth %, switch to Growth view, and see how many humans you need at higher volume with AI vs without.
